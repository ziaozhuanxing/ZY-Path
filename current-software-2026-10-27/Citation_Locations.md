## [1] Bangor (2009)

[1] Bangor, A., Kortum, P., & Miller, J. (2009). Determining what individual SUS scores mean: Adding an adjective rating scale. Journal of Usability Studies, 4(3), 114–123.

- 1.3 Hypothesis；段落 3；PDF 第 13 页／印刷第 4 页；prose。
  - 原文：For the planned pilot, SUS provides a descriptive measure of perceived usability; its interpretation will use the actual sample, tasks and score distribution (Brooke, 1996; Bangor et al., 2009). Hypothesis assessment combines the technical evidence with the corresponding user evidence rather than treating software tests as responses to a usability survey.
- 2.2.7 Human-centred design and evaluation；段落 2；PDF 第 20 页／印刷第 11 页；prose。
  - 原文：The System Usability Scale is a ten-item questionnaire for perceived usability (Brooke, 1996). Bangor et al. (2009) provide an interpretive framework relating SUS scores to adjective ratings. These sources support the planned instrument and contextual interpretation, while task outcomes and qualitative observations explain which interactions influence the score.

## [2] Bankhead (2017)

[2] Bankhead, P., et al. (2017). QuPath: Open source software for digital pathology image analysis. Scientific Reports, 7, article 16878. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-17204-5

- 1.1 Background and Motivation；段落 4；PDF 第 11 页／印刷第 2 页；prose。
  - 原文：Existing pathology platforms offer graphical inspection and analysis. QuPath provides an extensible image-analysis environment (Bankhead et al., 2017). Slideflow Studio supports whole-slide visualisation (Dolezal et al., 2024). WSInfer integrates reusable pretrained models with QuPath (Kaczmarzyk et al., 2024). ZY-Path addresses a focused workflow within this landscape by connecting supported local model loading, distinct analysis modes, spatial inspection and persistent inference records in one desktop application.
- 2.3.1 QuPath；段落 1；PDF 第 21 页／印刷第 12 页；prose。
  - 原文：QuPath is an open-source digital-pathology application with image visualisation, annotation, quantitative analysis and project-oriented workflows (Bankhead et al., 2017). It already provides a graphical interface and extensibility through scripts and extensions. Scripting is relevant to advanced customisation but is not a prerequisite for every interaction. Its whole-slide and annotation capabilities exceed the current scope of ZY-Path.

- 2.2.5 No-code interaction and transparent constraints；段落 1；PDF 第 20 页／印刷第 11 页；prose。
  - 原文：No-code deployment removes the need to write inference commands, but it does not remove the need for valid inputs. Its emphasis on model sharing and a standardised interface is directly relevant to the compatibility and provenance requirements of ZY-Path. It also demonstrates that no-code model use is not a unique invention of this capstone.

## [5] Kather (2019)

[5] Kather, J. N., et al. (2019). Predicting survival from colorectal cancer histology slides using deep learning: A retrospective multicenter study. PLOS Medicine, 16(1), e1002730. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1002730

- 1.1 Background and Motivation；段落 2；PDF 第 10 页／印刷第 1 页；prose。
  - 原文：Histopathology is a reference method for diagnosing many diseases, including malignancies. In haematoxylin and eosin (H&E) images, the staining of nuclei and cytoplasmic structures provides morphological information that trained professionals interpret in context. Digital imaging makes these observations available for storage, teaching and computational analysis. A model's output, however, is an inference from image appearance and must not be equated with a verified clinical diagnosis (Litjens et al., 2017; Kather et al., 2019). Figure 1.1 is reserved for an authorised H&E example illustrating this tissue context.
- 2.2.1 Digital histopathology and the unit of analysis；段落 2；PDF 第 17 页／印刷第 8 页；prose。
  - 原文：NCT-CRC-HE-100K contains 100,000 colorectal tissue patches across nine categories, while CRC-VAL-HE-7K provides a separate labelled evaluation collection (Kather et al., 2019). This project used the former for training and internal validation and the latter for the recorded 7,180-image test. The actual split manifests and measured results are described in Sections 3.3.2 and 4.2.6.

## [8] Yen (2012)

[8] Yen, P.-Y., & Bakken, S. (2012). Review of health information technology usability study methodologies. Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association, 19(3), 413–422. https://doi.org/10.1136/amiajnl-2010-000020

- 2.2.7 Human-centred design and evaluation；段落 1；PDF 第 20 页／印刷第 11 页；prose。
  - 原文：Usability inspection and empirical user evaluation answer complementary questions. Inspection can expose inconsistent feedback or inaccessible controls, whereas task observation and questionnaires show how intended users actually experience the system (Yen & Bakken, 2012). Nielsen's heuristics support visible status, user control, consistency, error prevention and recognition rather than recall (Nielsen, 1994).
- 3.4.1 Principles and information hierarchy；段落 2；PDF 第 43 页／印刷第 34 页；prose。
  - 原文：Task and classification mode are visible before running. Status messages distinguish validation, inference, cancellation and completion, while completed-result metadata remains separate from a pending selection. These decisions apply visibility, consistency, recognition and user control from the usability literature (Nielsen, 1994; Yen & Bakken, 2012); their effect on perceived usability will be assessed through the planned UAT.

## [9] Guo (2017)

[9] Guo, C., Pleiss, G., Sun, Y., & Weinberger, K. Q. (2017). On calibration of modern neural networks. In Proceedings of the 34th International Conference on Machine Learning, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research, 70 (pp. 1321–1330). https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html

- 2.2.4 Tiled classification, coverage and confidence；段落 2；PDF 第 19 页／印刷第 10 页；prose。
  - 原文：The model's softmax output is a normalised score over its classes. It is not automatically calibrated as the probability that an interpretation is correct (Guo et al., 2017). A high-confidence majority class across many tiles can arise from genuine image content, sampling scale, preprocessing mismatch or model/domain limitations. ZY-Path exposes per-tile probabilities and coordinates so these possibilities can be investigated rather than using a coloured map as proof of accuracy. Figure 2.1 distinguishes the three output meanings: whole-image classification, tile-class estimates and pixel segmentation.

## [10] He (2016)

[10] He, K., Zhang, X., Ren, S., & Sun, J. (2016). Deep residual learning for image recognition. In Proceedings of the IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (pp. 770–778). https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR.2016.90

  - 原文：Architecture selection and application integration answer different questions. Residual networks and EfficientNet provide useful comparative foundations (He et al., 2016; Tan & Le, 2019), while ZY-Path concentrates on reconstructing supported models from metadata and presenting their outputs under explicit contracts. The project evaluates its chosen classifier rather than conducting an architecture benchmark.
